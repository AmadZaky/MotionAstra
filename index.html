CURVE PRESETS
How to apply

Select two or more adjacent keyframes in AE, then Apply Curve. Handles change temporal easing; key times, values and motion paths stay in place. The graph shows normalized progress, not AE's raw speed graph.

Supports scalar, 2D and 3D properties. Roving keys and active expressions are skipped. For curved Position paths use zero endpoint speed (Out progress 0, In progress 1), or separate dimensions for custom endpoint speeds.

Drag a handle or focus it and use arrow keys; Shift makes larger steps. Only intervals whose two endpoints are selected are changed.

ANIMATION BY YUGraphic

YU Txt Motion

120 original text presets · by YUGraphic · MIT licensed

Original YUGraphic timing: IN starts at the layer edge; OUT ends one frame before the out-point. IN + OUT always uses layer edges. Short layers automatically fit the animation. Settings apply when you click Apply or Update. Text color and font stay unchanged.

Only YU IN/OUT animators are replaced or removed. Use Undo to restore. Canvas previews illustrate motion; AE typography and blur may render differently.
